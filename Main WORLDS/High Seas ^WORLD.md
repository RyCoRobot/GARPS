^WORLD
Game starts 5/27/988
	In high seas the world is made mostly of water, the rest being small(0.7 to 5 square miles) to middling(7 to 22 square miles) size islands. Middling Islands normally have docks and towns, but small ones may not. With Victorian level tech.

	 MAGIC
	 	There are three basic elements. Each person is born with the power to influence one. The force someone can exert is based on their training. The ways that people use these power is unique for everyone. The more power being used at once the more the negative effect activates. Negative effects ware off over time, but so do the positive ones.
		The elements are
		Sea: Control saltwater and with focus fish.  Slows the mind on use.
			Newbies - Can make small ripples.
			Masters - Conjure large waves and control wales.
		Storm: Control wind and weather. Makes user snappy and irational.
			Newbies - Can make small breezes and maybe a spark.
			Masters - Create storms and call lightning at will.
		Weight : Control the gravitational pull on items nearby, effectively making them lighter or heavier. The larger harder it is to do this. During use the user losses situational awareness
			Newbies - Can add or subtract 2% or 3% gravity.
			Masters - Multiples gravity 10X at least and can create reverse gravity.
			HISTORY
			About 988 years ago a monster came from the depths and used its magic to sink the planet to were it is today. Before being banished back to the deeps. His name was Droowin. NOT MANY KNOW OF THIS LOST SECRET
			About 75 years ago the totalitarian Pacificarian empire began their conquest and after a hard fought war they conquered the world with their superior tech.
			I the year 982 a rebellion began on Lerrest and was defeated by the Pacificarian Gaurd. I a hugely destructive battle where only one man survived, an insanely powerful storm mage called Elcstrabar. The destruction of Lerrest led to more rebellions, which joined the pirates and are trying to overthrow Pacificar.
		FACTIONS
		Pacificar: An authoritarian world government, led by the high general. Bent on peace, order, and bravery.
		The Pirates : A rag-tag band of rebels nomadicly sailing the seas without many bases, and trying to overthrow the government. They value freedom, strength, and  personal success.
		The Cult of Ancients: A shadowy cult bent on resummoning the creature that sank the world. (This is very hard to do and needs info from the center) They do not care for conflicts only the end. They also get abyss magic that replaces their element, and allows then to disintegrate things at the cost of their soul.
